# Voice Experience — Phase 16

**Status:** PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED
**Date:** 2026-08-11

## What Exists
- Mic button in AI chat activity for speech-to-text input
- Text-to-speech for AI responses (toggle with speaker button)
- Voice conversation mode (speak, AI responds, auto-listens again)

## What Phase 16 Requires (Not Yet Built)
- ⬜ Voice questions from any screen (only in chat activity)
- ⬜ Spoken navigation
- ⬜ Spoken research queries from map or record views
- ⬜ Voice confirmation before important actions
- ⬜ Prevention of destructive actions from ambiguous voice input

## Limitation
Voice is confined to the AI chat activity. No voice activation from other screens.
