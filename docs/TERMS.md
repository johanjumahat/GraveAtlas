# Terms of Use

**Last updated:** 2026-08-11
**Status:** Production-ready for Google Play Store

## Overview

These terms govern the use of GraveAtlas, a public cemetery and grave record platform.

## Acceptable Use

### You May:
- Search and browse cemetery and grave records
- Submit new grave or cemetery records
- Submit corrections to existing records
- Report inaccurate or inappropriate content
- Use the app for personal, educational, or research purposes
- Share links to public records

### You May Not:
- Submit false or misleading information
- Submit content that violates applicable law
- Submit content that disrespects the deceased or their families
- Attempt to bypass moderation or admin controls
- Scrape or bulk-download data without respecting rate limits
- Use the API for abusive or automated purposes
- Submit content that infringes on others' privacy rights
- Submit copyrighted photos without permission
- Submit personal information not publicly visible on grave markers
- Vandalize or promote vandalism of grave markers

## Data Contributions

- All submissions are reviewed by moderators before publication
- Contributors acknowledge that submitted content will become public
- Contributors are responsible for the accuracy of their submissions
- Contributors should not submit information about living persons without consent
- Contributors must declare image rights for all photos submitted
- Contributions are licensed under CC-BY-SA unless otherwise specified

## Community Standards

1. **Respect:** All content must be respectful of the deceased and their families
2. **Accuracy:** Contributors should submit accurate information from verifiable sources
3. **Transparency:** All data includes source attribution and verification status
4. **Collaboration:** Corrections and improvements are encouraged
5. **Privacy:** No private information beyond what's on public grave markers

See `docs/CONTENT-POLICY.md` for the full content policy.

## Moderation

- Moderators review all submissions before publication
- Moderators can approve, reject, or request changes
- Moderation decisions are final but can be appealed via the reporting system
- Repeated policy violations may result in account deactivation

## Correction Policy

- Any user can submit a correction to any record
- Corrections go through the same moderation review as new submissions
- Original data is preserved in the audit trail (never hard-deleted)
- Approved corrections update the published record

## Data Accuracy

GraveAtlas does not guarantee the accuracy of all records. Records may contain errors. The `verificationStatus` field indicates the level of verification:
- `unverified` — No verification performed
- `community_submitted` — Submitted by a community member
- `under_review` — Being reviewed by a moderator
- `verified` — Verified by a moderator or admin
- `rejected` — Found to be inaccurate

Users can submit corrections, which are reviewed by moderators.
