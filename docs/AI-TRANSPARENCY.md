# AI Transparency — Phase 16

**Status:** PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED
**Date:** 2026-08-11

## What Exists
- AI system prompt explicitly instructs evidence labeling
- AI states when it doesn't know
- AI mentions data is community-curated and may need verification
- AI provider and model shown in chat UI (provider/model spinner)
- Timestamps on chat messages

## What Phase 16 Requires (Not Yet Built)
- ⬜ "Why am I seeing this?" feature for AI results
- ⬜ Sources used by AI (AI doesn't have database access)
- ⬜ Records used by AI
- ⬜ Evidence category label on AI responses
- ⬜ Uncertainty indicator on AI responses
- ⬜ AI workflow/model information disclosure

## Limitation
Since the AI does not have direct database access, it cannot cite specific GraveAtlas records or sources in its responses. It reasons from general knowledge and the context provided.
