# Kubur AI — Changelog

## [1.0] — 2026-08-09

### Initial Release

- Created from AI Signal Pro (removed all trading features)
- AI chat as the main page
- Multi-provider support: Pollinations, Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter, Cerebras, Mistral, DeepSeek, Together AI, SambaNova
- Voice conversation mode with auto-listen loop
- Text-to-speech with voice selection
- Chat history persistence
- Auto-fallback across models and providers
- No trading, signals, alerts, or risk management features
